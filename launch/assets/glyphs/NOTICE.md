# Controller glyphs

Curated subset (Xbox / PlayStation / Switch families, "Default" style) of the owner-supplied archive `Source.zip`
(folders XGamepad, P5Gamepad, SGamepad). Files are renamed to a neutral button key (a, b, x, y, lb, rb, lt, rt, lstick, rstick,
l3, r3, back, start, dpad_*); pixel content is unmodified (128x128, scaled down at runtime).
