# Ragdoll Rally — Product History

## September 1, 2025
Ragdoll Rally began as a Matter.js / CodePen physics prototype. The initial working build contained an articulated ragdoll launched from a slingshot into a physics playground containing trampolines, explosive cubes, a booster pad, a target area, HUD/controls, and collision effects.

## September 2, 2025
Development moved toward a phone-installable build. The project gained camera-follow and scrolling/parallax presentation, mobile/landscape handling, scoring, persistent high scores, player initials, selectable Neon/Vaporwave/Arcade themes, settling adjustments, and a Capacitor-ready web layout with offline Matter.js support and CDN fallback.

The intended packaging target was Android/APK through Capacitor and Android Studio.

## 2026 rediscovery
The project was rediscovered during Hot Attic Games portfolio archaeology. Its original concept remains relevant: intentionally ridiculous ragdoll physics, launch/impact spectacle, environmental hazards, destruction, and score-chasing.

This document records only material positively recovered from project history. It does not claim later concepts were present in the September 2025 implementation unless explicitly stated.
