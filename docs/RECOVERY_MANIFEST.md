# Ragdoll Rally — Recovery Manifest

## Status

## Confirmed project identity
**Ragdoll Rally (Prototype)** — a mobile-oriented Matter.js physics game built first in CodePen and later prepared for Capacitor/Android packaging.

## Confirmed recovered design/functionality
- Slingshot-launched articulated ragdoll
- Trampolines
- Explosive cubes
- Booster pads
- Target/landing zone
- Collision-driven effects
- Reset/new-ragdoll controls and HUD
- Camera follow
- Scrolling/parallax city/background treatment
- Neon, Vaporwave, and Arcade selectable themes
- Scoring
- Persistent high scores
- Player initials
- Landscape/mobile handling
- Offline Matter.js loading with CDN fallback
- Capacitor-oriented Android packaging
- Historical downloadable-project layout: web/index.html, web/style.css, web/game.js, optional web/lib/matter.min.js
